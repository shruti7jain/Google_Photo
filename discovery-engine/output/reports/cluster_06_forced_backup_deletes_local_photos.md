# Insight Report: Forced backup deletes local photos

## Opportunity Score: 45.9/100
- **Volume Reach:** 58 user reviews (2.1 volume score)
- **Severity Impact:** 89.7% critical pain point rating
- **Primary Failure Mode:** `data_loss`
- **Primary Retrieval Type:** `object_based`

---

## The Problem in One Line
Users want to keep their pictures on the device and decide if/when they are backed up, but Google Photos forces backup and automatically removes local copies (e.g., via “Free up space” or hidden syncing). This unexpected behavior leads to data loss and makes the app feel un‑installable.

## Who Is Affected
- **Affected User Corpus:** 58 analyzed verbatim complaints.
- **Source Breakdown:** **App Store**: 2, **Play Store**: 56.

## Where It Breaks Down
- **Primary Failure Mode:** `data_loss`
- **Experience Gap:** Users attempt natural retrieval or organization patterns, but encounter friction due to keyword inflexibility, missing categorization options, or degraded search confidence.

## What Users Try (and Fail)
- **Typical Behaviors:** Approximate timeline scrolling, repetitive keyword guessing, album folder scanning, and manual workarounds.
- **Common Outcome:** High frustration, giving up on retrieval, or relying on external cloud/gallery solutions.

## Opportunity Statement
> **Product Opportunity:** High concentration of users (58 reports) experience retrieval breakdown under `data_loss` when attempting `object_based` photo retrieval. Improving semantic search tolerance and navigation controls here directly addresses customer dissatisfaction and boosts daily retrieval success rate.
