## The Problem in One Line
Users attempt to launch Google Photos to view, edit, or delete their pictures, but the app repeatedly crashes, gets stuck in permission loops, or displays error messages that block any access. Consequently, they cannot see or manage their photo library at all.

- **Common Outcome:** High frustration, giving up on retrieval, or relying on external cloud/gallery solutions.
